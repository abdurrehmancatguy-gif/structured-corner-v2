bak.png arrived with a checkerboard painted into the pixels: an RGB image with
no alpha channel, so it only looked transparent. The checkerboard was keyed
out (neutral light grey connected to the border) and the tin cropped tight to
its edges, so it can stand in front of its circle ("cutout": true in
navigation.json). 204x240, 256 colours.

The Pexels images are placeholders for BGS Corner's own photography.

corporate.png arrived the same way, a painted checkerboard in an RGB image. The
building's edges are straight, so the key is a polygon fitted to them (flat roof
and two sides, each within about a pixel). The photo looks steeply up at the
tower, which made it far wider at the bottom than a circle can hold, so its
perspective was partly straightened (roof 36% of the base instead of 19%) and
it was cut on a window line for a flat base. It stands on the disc like the
Bakhoor tin. 204x240, 256 colours.

attars.png and edp.png arrived the same way, painted checkerboards in RGB
images. The attar one's checkerboard is warped and as grey as the chrome, so it
was keyed by texture: the checkerboard flips light and dark every few pixels,
chrome is smooth, and the bottles are outlined in dark. The EDP bottle is
the whole Pride of Arabia bottle, cap and base included. Both are normalised to 204x240 like the tin, so all
the breakouts rise and stand alike. 256 colours.

gift-sets.png (2026-09-11) arrived the same way, a painted checkerboard with soft
white wisps over it, and the white ribbon bows meet the white squares. Keyed by
texture like the attars (the checkerboard flips every 8.5px, satin is smooth),
with enclosed pockets of checkerboard removed and the edges closed and opened
by a few pixels. The two boxes are a wide group, so it is not normalised to
204x240: it is cropped tight (300x154) and marked "cutout": "wide", which draws
it across its circle instead of rising out of it. 256 colours.
