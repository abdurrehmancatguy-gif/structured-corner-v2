Questions

Read the policies →
Where do you deliver?

Within the United Arab Emirates only. We do not ship outside the UAE.

How long does it take?

Orders are dispatched in 1 to 3 business days. After that, 1 to 2 business days in Dubai, 2 to 4 in the other emirates and 3 to 7 in remote areas.

What does delivery cost?

Free over AED 150, and AED 12 below that. What you pay is shown at checkout before you order.

How can I pay?

By card and the online payment options shown at checkout. We do not offer cash on delivery.

Can I return an order?

Yes, within 14 calendar days of delivery, unused and in its original packaging, with proof of purchase. Used or altered products cannot be returned.

How do I follow my order?

You get a dispatch email with a tracking link where one is available. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to go live.

Is VAT added?

No. We are not registered for VAT, so no VAT is added at checkout or on delivery.

Are the ouds alcohol-free?

The oud attars are oil based and alcohol-free. Each batch carries its own number.

Subscribe for discounts

Offers, new blends and early access, by email. One message at a time, and you can leave whenever you like.
