Shipping and Delivery Policy

Last Updated: 17 September 2026

This Shipping and Delivery Policy explains how BGS Corner General Trading L.L.C ("we," "us," or "our") processes, ships, and delivers orders placed through https://bgscorner.com (the "Website").

This policy forms part of our Terms and Conditions. By placing an order on the Website, you agree to the terms set out in this policy.

1. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to all orders placed through the Website for delivery within the United Arab Emirates. We currently do not ship to addresses outside the UAE. If we expand our delivery areas in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

2. Delivery Areas

We currently deliver only within the United Arab Emirates. We do not serve customers outside the UAE at this time. We reserve the right to expand our delivery areas in the future as part of our business expansion plans. Any such expansion will be announced on the Website and reflected in an updated version of this policy.

3. Order Processing Time

All orders are subject to a processing period before dispatch. Our standard order processing time is 1 to 3 business days (Saturday to Thursday, excluding public holidays in the UAE).

Orders placed on Fridays or during UAE public holidays will be processed on the next business day.

Once your order has been processed, you will receive a dispatch confirmation email with tracking details, where available.

4. Delivery Timeframes

Estimated delivery timeframes after dispatch are as follows:

  • ​ Dubai and surrounding areas: 1 to 2 business days.
  • ​ Other Emirates (Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, Umm Al Quwain): 2 to 4 business days.
  • ​ Remote or rural areas within the UAE: 3 to 7 business days.

These timeframes are estimates only and are not guaranteed. Actual delivery times may vary depending on your location, courier capacity, weather conditions, or other factors beyond our control.

5. Shipping Costs

Shipping costs, if any, will be clearly displayed at checkout before you complete your purchase. No additional shipping or logistics fees will be imposed after checkout.

From time to time, we may offer free shipping promotions. The terms of any such promotion will be displayed on the Website.

6. Payment Methods

We accept the following payment methods:

  • ​ Credit and Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard).
  • ​ Secure online payment gateways.

We do not offer Cash on Delivery.

We are not currently registered for Value Added Tax (VAT) and therefore do not charge VAT on any orders. No VAT will be added at checkout or on delivery.

7. Order Tracking

Where available, we will provide you with a tracking number and a link to track your order once it has been dispatched. You can also contact us at info@bgscorner.com or +971 58 929 7556 for updates on your order status.

Please allow up to 24 hours for tracking information to become active after you receive your dispatch confirmation.

8. Failed Delivery Attempts

If our courier is unable to deliver your order because no one is available to receive it, or because the delivery address provided is incorrect or inaccessible, the courier may:

  • ​ Attempt delivery again on another business day; or
  • ​ Return the order to us.

If the order is returned to us due to a failed delivery attempt, we will contact you to arrange redelivery. Additional shipping charges may apply for redelivery. If you choose not to have the order redelivered, we will refund the product price minus any original shipping costs and any return shipping costs incurred by us.

9. Delivery Address Accuracy

You are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address at checkout. Please include:

  • ​ Building name or number.
  • ​ Street name.
  • ​ Area or district.
  • ​ Emirate.
  • ​ Any additional landmarks or delivery instructions.

We are not responsible for delays or failed deliveries caused by incorrect or incomplete address information provided by you. If a delivery fails due to an incorrect address, additional charges may apply for redelivery.

10. Risk and Title

Risk of loss or damage to the products passes to you upon delivery. Title to the products passes to you upon full payment being received by us.

If you receive a product that is damaged in transit, please contact us immediately at info@bgscorner.com or +971 58 929 7556, and we will assist you in accordance with our Returns and Refund Policy.

11. Delivery Delays

We are not liable for any delay or failure to deliver caused by events beyond our reasonable control, including but not limited to:

  • ​ Extreme weather conditions.
  • ​ Natural disasters.
  • ​ Strikes or labour disputes.
  • ​ Courier service disruptions.
  • ​ Government actions, customs delays, or regulatory restrictions.
  • ​ Pandemics or public health emergencies. If such an event occurs, we will contact you as soon as reasonably possible to inform you of the delay and to arrange a new delivery date.

12. International Shipping

We currently do not offer international shipping. All orders must be delivered to an address within the United Arab Emirates.

If we begin offering international shipping in the future, this policy will be updated to include applicable terms, including any customs duties, taxes, or import fees that may apply. Any such charges will be the responsibility of the customer unless otherwise stated.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Shipping and Delivery Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our operations, courier partners, legal requirements, or business practices. We will post the updated version on this page and update the "Last Updated" date at the top.

Where changes are material, we will provide reasonable notice on the Website or by email before the changes take effect. Your continued use of the Website after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
